## Chronology / stop-before warnings

1. Aqueduct and Highgate introduction/dialogue cross-talk (#182–212); note, two Waypoints
2. Dried Lake: Voll and Kira: Deshret **#220 before freeing Deshret**; then the **#221–222** after-rescue dialogues, and Side Quest ID 9
3. Crystal Veins: Dialla Kaom/Daresso **#223–224 before finishing both boss lines**; Malachai's Notebook, Waypoint
4. Grand Arena: plaques and Waypoint; **#231–232 after Daresso before Kaom's Dream** in Steam 3.28 source path
5. Kaom's Dream / Stronghold: four Weathered Carvings and Waypoint; after Kaom / Rapture Device check **#233–239** before Belly of the Beast
6. Belly of the Beast: Tasuni Ghasts; **#241 Piety: The Godless Three before Harvest**; **#245 Tasuni: Malachai's Apprentices before Malachai**
7. Harvest: Waypoint, Malachai's Dedication; **#247 Piety: Malachai before handing in all organs**; **#248 Dialla: Malachai after the hand-in but before killing Malachai**
8. After Malachai: **#249–253** town dialogues; The Ascent Resonator Instructions

The route is divided into 13 UI steps for separate decisions, not eight screens. A STOP caution references only dialogue IDs within its phase; some other dialogue entries in the same chapter unlock *after* an event, so a user should follow each row's individual conditions instead of requiring all chapter cards before proceeding.
