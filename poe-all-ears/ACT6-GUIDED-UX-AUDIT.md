## Exact before/after quest decisions

1. **The Father of War**: hear Sin and Bestel on the way to Tukohama (#318–320), including Bestel before killing Tukohama.
2. **Eye of Conquest**: after obtaining it, hear Tarkleigh: The Eye of Conquest (**#321**) BEFORE using the item.
3. **After the Eye was used**: return to Tarkleigh for **The Karui Fortress (#322)** in sometimes-hidden quest dialogue BEFORE entering; also check Karui Revenants (#323). These are separate states, not duplicates.
4. **After Tukohama, before Shavronne**: hear Bestel: Shavronne of Umbra (#324) and Tarkleigh (#325).
5. **After Shavronne**: hear Sin: Shavronne (#326) before leaving The Prison, a historic source caution, then Tarkleigh's post-completion topic (#327).
6. **The Puppet Mistress**: hear Sin about Ryslatha (#330–331) before killing that boss.
7. **The Cloven One**: hear Sin before entering the Valley of the Fire Drinker (#328–329). Note local IDs do not match Wiki row sequence for these topics.
8. **After meeting Nessa**: talk Bestel and Tarkleigh about The Ship Graveyard (#332–333), especially Tarkleigh before The Cavern of Anger.
9. **The Black Flag**: hear Lilly Roth (#336) before burning the flag at The Beacon.
10. **Brine King's Reef**: hear both Weylam Roth topics (**#338 and #339**) BEFORE defeating Tsoagoth.
11. **After Brine King**: return to town for Lilly and Bestel (#340–341).

These warnings are historical, **not verified to be current or permanently missable on patch 3.30**. The old Act 6 six-checkpoint Wiki panel stays accessible as supporting evidence.

## UX/UI improvements

- **Active quest first**: source-led step, explicit STOP, actions, character-specific counters and relevant four-achievement rows appear before the duplicated Act-wide overview.
- Act-wide overview remains under a labelled collapsible disclosure; no checklist rows removed.
- Compact 2–4 category jump controls navigate directly to the matching row group, opening Lore / Waypoint / sidequest disclosures as needed. Users can navigate by keyboard as with other buttons.
- Expanded per-stage Lore, Waypoint and Sidequest panels now remain open after marking a record; the UI no longer requires reopening a group after each checkbox.
- Thai or English route actions and stop reasons are authored for all 15 Act 6 stages. The independent name modes use English source terms, editorial Thai translations or both.
- Lilly Roth display spelling normalized from historical "Lily Roth" source alias without changing stored IDs or raw source data.
- Two original quest-state conceptual SVG images for **The Father of War** (before entering Karui Fortress) and **The Brine King** (Weylam's two pre-boss conversations). Total illustrations: 13. Every diagram is visibly labelled **NOT AN IN-GAME SCREENSHOT**.
